I made a small game about rent.

Not the apartment kind — the other kind. The fee on the thing you already own, the subscription to your own stuff, the toaster with opinions. You swipe left or right through a month of it, and at the end you get a receipt.

Five minutes, in your browser, and nothing you do in it leaves the tab.

Play it at [grabsburg.mltru.com](/go/grabsburg).
