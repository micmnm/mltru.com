You swipe left or right through a month of fees: the subscription to your own stuff, the fee on the thing you already own, the toaster with opinions.

It takes five minutes. Probably less than cancelling a subscription.

A friend and I built it as an experiment: starting from scratch, how far can current AI models stretch creatively on fees, services and data?

Play it at [grabsburg.mltru.com](/go/grabsburg).
